Every repository is reproducible from seed.

Surrogate Modelling for Industrial Setpoint Optimisation

Response-surface model with calibrated uncertainty for scoring equipment setpoint changes before they are applied. Ranking candidates by predicted mean is net-negative (true mean −3.38, 9/20 harmful); adding a k‑NN support check and a lower-confidence-bound rule turns it positive (+1.22). Grouped-by-asset validation against an oracle R² ceiling, conformal-calibrated intervals (0.80 coverage), and a reported negative result — ensemble disagreement was a weak error signal (r = 0.16) next to distance-to-training-support (r = 0.71)

Demand Forecasting with External Economic Signals

Monthly demand forecasting where customer economics drive purchases with a multi-month lead time — and a measurement of the three ways a forecasting backtest lies. Feature-timing leakage overstates accuracy by 12–14%; automated lag selection loses to a flat guess because persistent AR(1) drivers make lags weakly identified; segment-margin construction lifts coefficient sign consistency from 0.56 to 0.76 where collinear raw prices give coin flips. Includes rolling-origin backtests, recursive 6‑month forecasts with horizon-specific conformal bands, and a production plan

A/B Testing, Causal Inference & Geo Experiments

One toolkit spanning three situations, each validated against a known ground-truth effect. Randomised: z/t‑tests, SRM guard, power/MDE, CUPED. Confounded: propensity scores, balance diagnostics, IPW and doubly-robust AIPW — recovering $3.00 where the naive difference reads $6.87. Geo-level (when per-user attribution is impossible): market-randomised design with a time-based-regression counterfactual, plus a simulation power analysis showing the experiment was underpowered — a near-exact estimate that still fails at p = 0.060. Narrated notebook includes a direct test of double robustness and what unmeasured confounding does to a passing diagnostic
